Trap: Little cylinder sticking out of the ground that players can hook onto to descend with a rope into a hole in the dungeon. Once they are significantly down the way, the cylinder pops into the floor and drops them down.

~~Creature: Large fuzzy black creature. Walks on all fours with extremely long appendages. Doesn't seem to have any other features on its main body. Arched in a sort of "A" formation.~~

Religion: Separates people into children of one god or another. There is a multiplicity of how to understand the distinction between the two groups. With some advocating for violence, some wishing to break away, some looking for unification, and everything in between. A religion that is constantly self-contradicting.

Magic: Detect spells (detect evil, magic, invisible, etc). Purify (item, person, food, etc). Reversible spells

Nice dungeon prompts:
- This place is a gateway into a new, strange place. The theme is study, rejection, often described as Loud, Glass. The environment is indoors (Friendly, Flaming) and the danger is a creature.
- This place is a treasure horde untouched. The theme is hunger, rejection, often described as Loud, Calm. The environment is mountain (Desolate, Black) and the danger is a creature.
