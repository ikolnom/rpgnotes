Divine council of gods, who made first contact with humanity a few hundred years ago. Delivering the first inklings of magic into the hands of humanity. However, they have not been heard from since. Although the lesser deities and spirits still are able to be contacted and communicated with. Thus, intercession through these figures to the Adrhun is common. Most people believe there are at least nine figures in the council. Although numbers vary as some discredit others beliefs and raise their own figures up as members of the Adrhun.
