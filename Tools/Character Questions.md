1. What is this persons name and physical characteristics?
2. Why are they where they are?
3. What is their general disposition? Trusting? Cunning? Violent?
4. What is their current passion? Why do they get up each day?
5. What do they do for a living? Do they enjoy this work? Did they freely choose it?
6. Do they have family? Friends they care deeply for? A lover?
7. What ties do they have? A religion, faction, guild, kingdom?
8. What do they fear the most?
9. What has hurt them deeply in life?
10. When were they happiest?
