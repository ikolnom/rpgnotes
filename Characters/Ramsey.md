Father of [[Starlie]] and head of [[Sannach]] along with his brother [[Oyst]]. Whereas his brother is more in charge of theological matters and running the priesthood, Ramsey is in charge of the institution of the Church. Bookkeeping, setting up loans and new parishes around the realm, dialogue with other faiths, etc.

Ramsey is a stout man, loud and uncompromising with a strong direction and attitude towards his work. Clean shaven and bald, never seen without his crown of horns which spiral out without pattern from his head. He wears largely unadorned clothes - the simple robes of the priesthood - although has a fixation on jewelry and accessories. Clearly enjoys the finer things in life, and has no real problem finding a justification for his lifestyle despite his calling in life. 

Ramsey chooses to live privately in the town of [[Ionah]], rather than the traditional throne in [[Mount Cylth]]. Some say out of a desire for wealth and privacy, but more than likely from a deep-seated anger towards his brother [[Oyst]]. He is currently overseeing a large project to expand the burgeoning city underneath the mount incorporating nearby towns. This is in an effort to maintain a net income and expand the religions influence across the land. Ramsey does genuinely have the interest of the faith in mind, yet has no qualms towards less than savory means to his ends.

He is on better terms with his sister [[Cythia]], who helped raise his son [[Starlie]] after the death of his wife. More than anything, he fears failing his generations of ancestors who have entrusted the mount to him. He has always felt second to his brother [[Oyst]], who was clearly his parents favorite, despite his best attempts to flaunt his own unique skills. Ramsey has never been quite as happy as when he and his siblings were all children, playing together with his future wife.
