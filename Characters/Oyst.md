Oyst stands nearly seven feet tall, gaunt and skeletal. His withered fingers almost gracing his knees, where his long grey hair falls. Adorned in a large, flowing robe to hide his rotting body. He is the spiritual head of [[Sannach]], along with his brother [[Ramsey]]. Scrupulous and distrusting, the throne has significantly altered him. Leading to him closing off from the rest of the clergy and even his own family. As the city encroaches upon the sacred altar at the zenith of [[Mount Cylth]], he has worked up a more and more extreme plan to rid the site of the corruption.

Oyst never truly desired the role he was placed into. Yet he always seemed to have a natural aptitude for the spiritual and theological. Thus, he came into favor of both his parents and the larger clerical world within [[Sannach]]. He, much like his brother, fears failing the religion he was put in charge of. The stress of being pulled in different directions by his passion, duty, and family has caused him such pain that he has forever shut himself off.
