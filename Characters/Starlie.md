Son of [[Ramsey]]. Highly precocious and eccentric, he can be found getting into trouble almost habitually. Has a tendency to escape from any situation he's in. Unbeknownst to him, he is at the center of a planned coup by [[Oyst]], where he is to be killed along with his father to form a succession crisis. Allowing Oyst to have complete control over the religion, and destroy the city being built beneath him.
