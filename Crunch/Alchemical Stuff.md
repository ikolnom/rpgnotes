There are a few different things a skilled alchemist can make
- Incense (effects everything around it)
- Oil (Can be applied to something for the effect or used in another recipe)
- Essence/Extract (extremely concentrated)
- Teas (Necessary for certain effects especially ones effecting cognition)
- Potions (Instantaneous effects, powerful but limited. Usually borderline inedible)
