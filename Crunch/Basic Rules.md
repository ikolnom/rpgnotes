Players start with +0 in all attributes, and 8HP. They choose 3 skills, one beginner, one intermediate and one advanced. Depending on background they may wish to start with some gear or randomly roll on a table or purchase them. Players can hold BODY + 8 items of reasonable size, and no more than BODY + 15. Anything after BODY + 8 results in a debuff of considerable proportions for being over encumbered. (More food/water eaten, move slower, rolls are harder)

Skills are either
(!) for in-progress/learning
(B) for beginner (+2)
(I) for intermediate (+4)
(A) for advanced (+6)
(E) for expertise (+8 AND Advantage)

Each level has three stages, which can be checked off when time is spent training in them from a teacher (abt a week) or on your own (takes about a month). You can only train one skill at a time. To get to an expert level, you must either spend one year per level on your own, seek out a true master to study under for a year, or seek divine counsel. At an expert level, you cannot critical fail.

A single torch (or oil flask) burns for about one hour. Each dungeon turn takes 10 minutes so a single d6 is used to keep track of torch level. After combat, regardless of how long it takes, 10 minutes pass to account for regrouping, minor healing, looting, etc.

For every hour spent in the dungeon, the party must rest for one hour or suffer a -2 penalty on all rolls until they rest again.

Becoming advanced in a skill, getting 10,000 gp, or at DM's discretion (such as the end of a long quest or dungeon) gives you a level. A level adds +1 to one major attribute (Body/Mind/Soul) up to +4 and d2+1 HP

references
- 1gp ~ $5
- middle-class salary is roughly 10,000gp per year
- body -> athleticism (speed, strength, constitution...)
- mind -> mental acuity (memory/recall, learning speed...)
- soul -> intuition (magic casting, persuasion, reading peoples intentions...)
