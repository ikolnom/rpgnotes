Origin: Farming/agriculture community
Priority: Entertainment (gambling, redlight, etc)
Age: ~50 years
Medium sized ~24,000 ppl
Outside the city:
- Resource harvesting (mining)
- wealthy family estate
Minimal stewardship
Decent condition, lightly fortified
Huge market square, total open market where vendors spill out onto surrounding streets and even outside the city walls in cases
Large sewer system beneath the city
The population is roughly average, although with the occasional spikes during festivals that out of city people may come visit
The populace is fairly varied, with people from all over Haffanlle and foreign lands represented here equally.
Most people live in destitution, scraping by if they even can at all.
Visitors often come to trade, do business, seek criminal activities, or see the festivals
Night life is huge here, in fact some places don't even open until long after the sun has gone down.
A mayor is elected every year
There is a decently organized city watch
Crime is fairly common, and in fact there is often talk of organized crime. Some things in the city (especially in the upper ranks) seem too convenient..
