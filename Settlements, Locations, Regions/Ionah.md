Satellite town to [[Mount Cylth]], private residence of [[Ramsey]]. Historically a trading hub and important restocking point along the main trade highway through the continent. 

District 1: Main Market
- Large area for restocking, trading, selling, and staying the night. Lots of traveling services for sale here. 
	- Large underground system of tunnels...
	- Small theater to house traveling troupes
District 2: Familial community
- Families that have been here for generations. Mostly serving the people passing through, but some find work in other areas.
	- Community graveyard
	- Hospital and apothecary
District 3: [[Sannach]] area
- Last stopping point for those on pilgrimage. Religion specific amenities
	- [[Ramsey]] estate
	- Small Sannach-loyal barracks
