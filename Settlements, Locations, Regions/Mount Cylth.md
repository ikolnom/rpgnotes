Holiest pilgrimage site of the [[Sannach]] faith. [[Annai]] resides deep in the heart of the mountain, accessible only by the upper echelons of the high priesthood. What started as a small village by the foot of the mountain to serve pilgrims has grown over the years into a sprawling city. Slowly encroaching upon the zenith of the mountain. Tensions have been flaring as of late, made worse by the discovery of a major gold and precious jewel vein within Cylth.
